Berlin,

Josselin’s work has graced magazines, billboards, album covers, and countless Instagram feeds, solidifying his reputation as a master of capturing life’s fleeting, unfiltered moments. Whether shooting models, musicians, celebrities, or close friends, his candid and cinematic style turns the ordinary into the extraordinary. His portfolio, marked by its raw intimacy and unmatched speed, stands as both personal and unmistakably distinctive.

A proud Leica ambassador and dedicated fashion enthusiast, Josselin embraces style over comfort with unapologetic flair. Known for thriving under intense time constraints, he consistently delivers striking visuals that resonate. His series of books showcases a deep love for storytelling, while his infamous production playlists—and his sharp, dark humor—add an edge to every project.
